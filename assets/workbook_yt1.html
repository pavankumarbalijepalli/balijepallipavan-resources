1. Why am I interested in AI?

3. What does this role expect from me?

Roles are not just jobs. Roles are a place where you take a problem and solve it.

Prompt 1, for Claude or ChatGPT

Search the web for job descriptions and public posts about the role of [your role name] at the [fresher, senior or founder] level, and then simulate my day as a person in that role, hour by hour. Show your sources. Do the SWOT analysis of the particular role and give me how it can clash with my lifestyle. If you are unsure of my lifestyle, assume potential clashes and list it down. I need the complete pros and cons for this role and what kind of a personality would better fit this role, and I want you to cross verify myself with this personality. So keep it clear and simple to understand.

Turn web search on first, and open the sources. The answer is the average of what others face. You will not face the exact same things.
